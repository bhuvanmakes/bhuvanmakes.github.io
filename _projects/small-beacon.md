Small Beacon is a compact LoRa beacon. The planned hardware is an ESP32-C3 Super Mini with a Reyax RYLR998 LoRa module.

It is featured here as work in progress. There is not yet a public repository, Instructable, Hackster page or video, so this page does not invent a listing. Details will be added as they are published.
